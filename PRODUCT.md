# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Polytechnic diploma graduates in Karnataka seeking lateral-entry (2nd year / 3rd semester) B.E. and B.Tech engineering admissions through the Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA DCET). Candidates typically face high anxiety, fragmented data across 200-page cutoff PDFs, predatory counseling agents, and opaque multi-quota reservation rules.

## Product Purpose
KEA Next (JanCET) transforms lateral-entry engineering admissions into a fast, transparent, and stress-free digital experience. It eliminates redundant manual form-filling, unlocks hidden multi-year cutoff trends across complex statutory quotas, and empowers candidates to make optimal, risk-proof college option entry decisions without paying third-party middlemen.

## Positioning
A zero-form, 4-identifier admission portal combining instant state registry verification (Aadhaar, SSLC, DTE USN, Nadakacheri RD numbers) with an interactive multi-year cutoff feasibility engine, automatic Supernumerary Quota (SNQ) and Article 371J fee calculations, and a deterministic Round 1 and Round 2 seat allotment simulator.

## Operating Context
Used on desktop and mobile web during high-stakes admission milestones (registration, document verification, mock allotment, and multi-round option entry lock-in). Must provide bulletproof reliability during peak load and transparent official document generation (Verification Slip with Secret Key, Provisional Allotment Letter, and Admission Order).

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Zero-form digital data retrieval via 4 core identifiers (Aadhaar, SSLC Roll No, Diploma USN, Caste/Income/371J RD Numbers).
- Auto-calculation of verified category codes (GM, 3AG, 2AG, SCG, 3AHK, SNQ).
- Real-time admission probability scoring (Safe, Moderate, Ambitious) based on 3-year historical cutoffs (2023 to 2025).
- Constraint-based filtering (branch mapping, district/city, max tuition fee budget slider, on-campus hostel availability).
- Interactive reorderable Option Entry priority studio with auto-optimization.
- Multi-round seat allotment simulator explaining Choice 1 (Freeze), Choice 2 (Hold & Upgrade), Choice 3 (Reject), and Choice 4 (Exit).
- Strict adherence to Sagar's Project Constitution v1 (Human-first, no AI blue or purple gradients, clean typography, accessible semantic HTML).

## Brand Commitments
- Official Government of Karnataka and Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA) authoritative identity.
- Clean public service design ethos: Slate and navy neutrals with functional emerald and amber accents.
- Zero promotional hype or manipulative dark patterns; complete fee and cutoff transparency.

## Evidence on Hand
- Authentic 10-college dataset with official Karnataka college codes, NAAC and NIRF rankings, lateral intake seats, placement records, and 3-year category cutoffs.
- 4 comprehensive demo student personas for evaluation and validation.

## Product Principles
1. Zero Redundancy: Never ask a student to type what a verified state registry already knows.
2. Transparent Feasibility: Demystify cutoff PDFs into clear, actionable probability ratings.
3. No Seat Left to Chance: Prevent option entry mistakes with automated priority balance audits.
4. Complete Cost Clarity: Highlight fee waivers (SNQ, Govt vs Private) before decisions are locked.
5. Predictable UX: Clearly visualize the exact consequences of Choice 1, 2, 3, and 4 before final submission.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High contrast typography meeting WCAG AAA guidelines.
- Keyboard navigation across forms, reorderable choice lists, and modal drawers.
- Fully responsive layout optimized for mobile smartphones, tablets, and desktops.
